What Is Knee Replacement Surgery?

Knee replacement surgery, also called knee arthroplasty, is a surgical procedure where the damaged or worn-out parts of the knee joint are removed and replaced with artificial implants, typically made of metal, plastic, or ceramic components, designed to replicate the smooth movement of a natural knee.

The surgery is most commonly recommended for patients dealing with:

  • Osteoarthritis - the most common cause of knee degeneration in people over 50
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis - an autoimmune condition causing painful joint inflammation
  • Post-Traumatic Arthritis - arising from injuries, fractures, or ligament damage
  • Knee Deformities - causing chronic pain and loss of functional movement

When medications, injections, and physiotherapy no longer provide relief, knee replacement becomes the most effective path forward.

Types of Knee Replacement Surgery Available in Hyderabad

Not every patient needs the same procedure. Depending on the extent of joint damage, your surgeon will recommend one of the following options:

1. Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

The most performed type. All three compartments of the knee joint are resurfaced with prosthetics. Ideal for patients with widespread arthritis affecting the entire knee.

2. Partial (Unicompartmental) Knee Replacement

Only the damaged compartment is replaced. Less invasive with a faster recovery, suitable for patients with arthritis limited to one area of the knee.

3. Bilateral Knee Replacement

Both knees are replaced in the same surgical session. Recommended only for patients in excellent overall health, it eliminates the need for a second procedure.

4. Robotic-Assisted Knee Replacement

A precision-driven approach where advanced robotic technology assists the surgeon in achieving perfect implant alignment. Results in less post-operative pain, faster recovery, and better long-term outcomes.

What Happens During Knee Replacement Surgery?

Here's a step-by-step breakdown of the procedure from start to finish:

  1. Pre-Surgical Evaluation - Blood tests, X-rays, ECG, and medical fitness clearance
  2. Anaesthesia - General or spinal anaesthesia is administered for comfort and safety
  3. Joint Exposure - A surgical incision is made to access the knee joint
  4. Bone Reshaping - Damaged cartilage and bone surfaces are carefully removed
  5. Implant Placement - Prosthetic components are fitted and secured with precision
  6. Wound Closure - The incision is closed and a temporary drain may be placed
  7. Post-Op Monitoring - Patient is observed closely in the recovery room for several hours

The entire surgery typically takes 1 to 2 hours. With robotic assistance, precision is significantly enhanced.

Recovery After Knee Replacement Surgery in Hyderabad

Recovery is a staged journey. Understanding what to expect at each phase helps patients stay motivated, follow their physiotherapy protocol, and set realistic milestones.

Timeline Phase What to Expect
Day 1 – 5 In-Hospital Pain managed with medication. Physio starts on Day 1. Standing with support within 24–48 hrs. Discharge in 3–5 days.
Week 1 – 6 Early Home Recovery Walking with a walker/crutches. Wound care, medication, daily physiotherapy. No driving for the first 4–6 weeks.
Week 6 – 12 Mid-Term Recovery Transition to independent walking. Return to low-impact activities (walking, swimming). Swelling gradually reduces.
Month 3 – 6 Full Recovery Return to normal daily activities, light exercise. High-impact sports and heavy lifting should be avoided long-term.

Expert Note on Physiotherapy After Knee Replacement in Hyderabad

Physiotherapy is not optional, it is the foundation of a successful recovery. Starting as early as Day 1 post-surgery, exercises rebuild muscle strength, restore range of motion, and reduce swelling. Most patients require physiotherapy for 3–6 months, and those who follow their rehab plan diligently experience significantly better long-term outcomes.

How to Prepare for Knee Replacement Surgery

A little preparation before surgery can make a big difference in how quickly and smoothly you recover.

  • Complete Medical Evaluation: Blood tests, ECG, X-rays, and cardiologist clearance if you have a heart condition
  • Medication Review: Blood thinners and anti-inflammatory drugs may need to be paused before surgery, inform your surgeon of all medications
  • Arrange Home Support: You'll need help with daily tasks for the first few weeks, plan for this in advance
  • Prepare Your Living Space: Remove trip hazards, add bathroom handrails, and keep essential items within easy reach
  • Pre-Surgery Exercises: Strengthening your leg muscles before the operation can speed up recovery significantly
  • Insurance Clearance: Get pre-authorization from your insurer or TPA before the admission date to avoid delays

Frequently Asked Questions: Knee Replacement in Hyderabad

What is the exact cost of knee replacement surgery in Hyderabad?

The cost ranges from ₹1.7 Lakhs to ₹3.5 Lakhs per knee. The final figure depends on the type of procedure (total, partial, or robotic), the implant selected, the hospital, and whether insurance is used.

How long does knee replacement surgery take?

The procedure typically takes 1 to 2 hours. Robotic-assisted surgeries may take slightly longer due to the precision-mapping phase but generally offer better outcomes.

How many days of hospital stay are required after knee replacement in Hyderabad?

Most patients stay in the hospital for 3 to 5 days. During this period, you will receive pain management, post-operative care, and begin your first physiotherapy sessions.

Is knee replacement surgery covered by health insurance in India?

Yes, most Indian health insurance policies cover knee replacement surgery. It is important to check your specific policy and get TPA or insurer pre-authorization before your admission date.

How long does physiotherapy after knee replacement in Hyderabad last?

Physiotherapy typically continues for 3 to 6 months, depending on individual recovery progress. Consistent physiotherapy is the single most important factor in achieving full functional recovery.

Is partial knee replacement cheaper than total knee replacement?

Yes, partial knee replacement generally costs less and involves a faster recovery. However, the decision is always based on the clinical extent of joint damage, not on cost alone.

How long do knee implants last after surgery?

Modern knee implants are designed to last 15 to 20 years. With advancements in implant materials and robotic placement techniques, longevity continues to improve.

Can both knees be replaced at the same time?

Yes, this is called bilateral knee replacement. It is recommended for patients who have arthritis in both knees and are in good overall health, eliminating the need for a second surgery.

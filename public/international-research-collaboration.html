International Collaboration

International Collaboration & Expertise

Explore the foundation of our global orthopedic collaboration, academic training, medical innovation, and patient-focused support network.

Step 1

Academic & Training Background

During his academic training in advanced orthopedics in Munich, Germany, Dr. Mir Jawad Zar Khan worked with internationally renowned orthopedic specialists and gained strong experience in joint replacements, arthroscopy, spine procedures, trauma care, and non-operative orthopedic treatment.

Step 2

International Recognition

Impressed by Dr. Mir Jawad Zar Khan's work and dedication toward patient care, international experts extended their collaboration to support the development of an advanced orthopedic, neuro, and multi-specialty hospital in Hyderabad.

Step 3

Research Foundation Collaboration

Dr. Mir Jawad Zar Khan and the international medical panel are collaborating to build a dedicated orthopedic and musculoskeletal research foundation, focused on arthroscopy, traumatology, joint surgeries, spine research, and sustainable long-term academic partnerships.

Step 4

Advanced Medical Services

This collaboration also supports advanced treatment planning, including specialized anesthetic techniques such as combined spinal epidural anesthesia and nerve blocks, helping deliver safer joint replacement care for elderly patients with coexisting medical conditions.

Step 5

Hospital Support & Visit Schedule

International specialists visit the hospital on a regular basis and support the medical team. The hospital is backed by experienced neurosurgeons, anesthesiologists, physicians, ICU support, and world-class operation theatres for advanced multidisciplinary care.

Step 6

Patient Information & Appointments

International doctor visit schedules will be published in advance so that local and international patients can book appointments easily with support from the hospital team. Patients can also connect with the international patient relations department for additional assistance.

Extracorporeal Shock Wave Therapy (ESWT)

Extracorporeal Shock Wave Therapy (ESWT) is a non-invasive treatment that uses high-energy acoustic waves to treat various musculoskeletal conditions.

At Germanten Hospitals, we utilize advanced ESWT technology to provide effective treatment for chronic pain conditions, promoting healing and tissue regeneration without the need for surgery or medications.

How ESWT Works

Shock Wave Generation

High-energy acoustic waves are generated and focused on the treatment area.

Tissue Stimulation

The waves stimulate blood flow and support the body’s natural healing process.

Pain Reduction

Treatment may reduce inflammation and interrupt chronic pain signals.

Conditions Treated

  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Achilles tendinitis
  • Tennis elbow
  • Golfer's elbow
  • Shoulder tendinitis
  • Calcific shoulder tendinitis
  • Bone stress fractures
  • Chronic muscle pain

Benefits of ESWT

  • Non-invasive procedure
  • No anesthesia required
  • Quick treatment sessions
  • Rapid pain relief
  • Minimal downtime
  • Long-lasting results

Spine Conditions Treated

Our spine department handles the full spectrum of spinal disorders affecting adults of all ages. The team evaluates and treats the following conditions with equal expertise and care.

  • Lumbar Disc Herniation (Slipped Disc): A herniated or slipped disc in the lower back puts pressure on the surrounding nerves, causing pain that travels down the leg in a pattern known as sciatica. This is one of the most common reasons patients visit a spine surgeon in Hyderabad.
  • Cervical Disc Disease: When discs in the neck degenerate or herniate, they can compress nerves or the spinal cord, leading to neck pain, arm pain, tingling, or weakness.
  • Spinal Stenosis: This condition involves a narrowing of the spinal canal, placing pressure on the spinal cord and nerves. It is more common in people over 50 and can cause significant difficulty walking, standing, or carrying out daily activities.
  • Spondylolisthesis: When one vertebra slips forward over the one below it, the result is chronic back pain and, in severe cases, nerve compression. Dr. Jawad uses advanced stabilisation techniques to address this condition effectively.
  • Vertebral Fractures: Fractures caused by trauma, osteoporosis, or accidents require urgent and precise intervention. Our team performs stabilisation and vertebroplasty procedures to restore spinal alignment and relieve pain.
  • Scoliosis and Kyphosis: Abnormal spinal curvatures require careful planning. Surgical correction is recommended in selected cases where the curve is severe or progressing rapidly.
  • Degenerative Disc Disease: The natural wear and tear of spinal discs over time can cause chronic pain. Our specialists assess severity and tailor treatment accordingly, whether conservative or surgical.

Surgical Treatment for Spine Problems

When conservative care fails to deliver adequate relief, surgical intervention becomes the appropriate next step. As the best spine surgeon in Hyderabad, Dr. Mir Jawad Zar Khan performs a wide range of spinal procedures using the latest technology and internationally validated techniques. Patients looking for detailed information on individual procedures can visit our dedicated spine surgical treatment page.

  • Microdiscectomy: This minimally invasive procedure removes the portion of a herniated disc that is pressing on a nerve root. It is one of the most effective solutions for sciatica caused by lumbar disc herniation and allows most patients to walk and return home within a day or two of surgery.
  • Spinal Fusion (Vertebral Arthrodesis): In cases of degenerative disc disease, spondylolisthesis, or spinal instability, vertebral fusion uses bone grafts along with metal implants such as screws, cages, and rods to permanently join two or more vertebrae. This eliminates painful movement between unstable segments.
  • Vertebroplasty and Kyphoplasty: These procedures are used for compression fractures, particularly in patients with osteoporosis. A specialised cement is injected into the fractured vertebra to stabilise it and provide immediate pain relief.
  • Laminectomy and Laminotomy: These decompression surgeries remove part of the lamina (the bony arch of the vertebra) to create more space within the spinal canal, relieving pressure on the spinal cord or nerve roots caused by stenosis.
  • Cervical Disc Replacement: In selected patients with cervical disc disease, an artificial disc is inserted in place of the damaged one, preserving natural neck movement while eliminating the source of pain.
  • Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery (MISS): For appropriate candidates, minimally invasive approaches use smaller incisions, reduce blood loss, cause less muscle damage, and result in shorter hospital stays and quicker recoveries compared to traditional open surgery.

Intraoperative Neurophysiological Monitoring (IONM)

One of the key features that sets our facility apart from other spine surgery centres in Hyderabad is the use of Intraoperative Neurophysiological Monitoring, commonly known as IONM. During complex spinal surgeries, IONM involves continuous real-time monitoring of the spinal cord and nerve function throughout the procedure. Dedicated neurophysiology technicians track signals from the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves, alerting the surgical team the moment any change is detected.

This technology serves as a critical safety net. It helps prevent nerve-related complications, allows the surgeon to make immediate intraoperative decisions, and gives patients and families the assurance that every precaution is being taken. Very few hospitals in Hyderabad offer IONM as a standard feature during spinal surgery, making this facility a genuinely differentiated choice for patients seeking the best spine surgery hospital in Hyderabad.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need spine surgery?

Surgery is recommended only when a specific structural problem is confirmed on imaging, conservative treatment has been given adequate time, and the symptoms are significantly affecting your daily life. Dr. Jawad conducts a thorough clinical and radiological assessment to guide every decision.

What makes this the best spine surgery hospital in Hyderabad?

The combination of an internationally trained surgeon, 3D C-Arm technology, surgical navigation, IONM monitoring, and a full multidisciplinary care team makes this one of the most capable spinal care centres in the region.

How long does recovery take after spine surgery?

Recovery varies by procedure. Minimally invasive procedures like microdiscectomy typically allow patients to walk within 24 hours. More complex fusion surgeries involve a structured physiotherapy programme that spans several weeks, with most patients returning to normal activity within three to six months.

Do you treat patients from outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Patients travel from across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, other Indian states, and even from abroad to consult with Dr. Jawad. Our international patient team provides dedicated coordination, travel assistance, and remote consultation support.

Can spine pain be treated without surgery?

In many cases, yes. Physiotherapy, targeted injections, ESWT, and other non-surgical approaches successfully manage spine pain. Surgery is only recommended when conservative care has been fully explored and has not provided sufficient relief.

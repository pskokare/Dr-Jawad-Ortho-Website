Know the Cause

What Causes Neck Pain?

The neck, or cervical spine, is a delicate structure of small bones, discs, joints, nerves and muscles that supports the head and allows it to move. Neck pain can arise from any of them, and identifying the exact source is the whole basis of effective treatment. This is why an accurate diagnosis by a qualified neck pain doctor in Hyderabad matters before any treatment begins. The most common causes we see are the following.

01

Cervical Spondylosis

Age-related wear of the cervical spine, and one of the most common causes of chronic neck pain and stiffness, often worse after long periods in one position.

02

Cervical Disc Herniation (Slip Disc in the Neck)

When a disc in the neck bulges and presses on a nerve, it causes neck pain that often radiates into the shoulder, arm or hand, along with tingling or numbness.

03

Poor Posture and Tech Neck

Long hours looking down at phones and laptops place continuous strain on the neck, a leading cause of neck pain among Hyderabad's IT and office workforce and increasingly in younger adults.

04

Muscle Strain

Sudden awkward movements, sleeping in a poor position or overuse can strain the neck muscles, causing pain and stiffness that limits movement.

05

Cervical Radiculopathy (Pinched Nerve)

When a nerve in the neck is compressed, it produces pain, weakness, tingling or numbness that travels down the arm, a pattern that needs proper assessment.

06

Whiplash and Neck Injuries

Sudden jolts from accidents or falls can injure the soft tissues and structures of the neck, causing pain that may appear immediately or over the following days.

07

Cervical Spinal Stenosis

A narrowing of the spinal canal in the neck that puts pressure on the spinal cord or nerves, causing pain and sometimes affecting the hands and walking.

08

Neck Pain With Swelling

Neck pain accompanied by a lump or swelling needs prompt evaluation to identify the cause. Patients searching for doctors for neck pain and swelling in Hyderabad should have this assessed without delay rather than waiting.

09

Inflammatory Conditions

Conditions such as rheumatoid and other inflammatory arthritis can affect the neck and are managed through our rheumatoid arthritis service.

Warning Signs

Symptoms That Need a Neck Pain Doctor

The following symptoms should prompt a visit to a neck pain specialist doctor in Hyderabad without delay. Early assessment leads to earlier and more effective treatment.

Neck pain that lasts more than a week or two and has not responded to rest, pain that radiates into the shoulder, arm or hand, tingling, numbness or a pins and needles sensation in the arm, weakness or clumsiness in the hand or grip, stiffness that severely limits neck movement, headaches that start at the base of the skull, neck pain following an accident or fall, and any neck pain accompanied by a lump or swelling are all indicators that a clinical assessment is overdue.

Seek urgent care: Neck pain with problems in coordination, difficulty walking, or loss of bladder or bowel control can signal serious pressure on the spinal cord and should be treated as an emergency.

Precise Assessment

How We Diagnose Neck Pain

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective neck pain treatment in Hyderabad at our centre. A precise diagnosis requires more than a single X-ray. The following evaluation is carried out at your first consultation.

A detailed clinical history covers when the pain started, where it is felt, whether it radiates into the arm, what makes it better or worse, and what it is limiting. A thorough physical examination assesses neck movement, posture, muscle strength, reflexes, nerve function and specific tests to identify the affected structure. Imaging begins with X-rays to assess the cervical bones and alignment, and progresses to MRI when detail on the discs, nerves and spinal cord is needed. Where swelling is present, additional imaging and tests are arranged to identify the cause.

Following the evaluation, every patient receives a clear, plain-language explanation of their diagnosis and a personalized treatment plan covering all available options before any decision is made.

Conservative Care First

Non-Surgical Neck Pain Treatment in Hyderabad

The large majority of neck pain is treated successfully without surgery, especially when addressed early. As the best hospital for neck pain in Hyderabad, our non-surgical programme is offered as a genuine first-line option, not as a formality before surgery is recommended. The following non-surgical treatments are available.

01

Structured Physiotherapy

A customized programme strengthens the muscles that support the neck, improves flexibility, corrects posture and addresses the movement and screen habits that cause neck pain. Physiotherapy is the single most effective non-surgical treatment for most causes of neck pain. Full details are on our non-surgical spine treatment page.

02

Medication and Activity Modification

Anti-inflammatory medication and pain relievers control symptoms during a flare, while guided posture and activity changes protect the neck and support recovery.

03

Image-Guided Injections

Cervical epidural injections and nerve root blocks, administered under imaging guidance for precision, reduce inflammation around a compressed nerve and provide significant relief for arm pain caused by a pinched nerve in the neck.

05

For chronic muscular and soft-tissue neck pain, ESWT delivers focused acoustic energy that stimulates healing without any surgical intervention.

06

Pain Management

Posture Correction and Ergonomics

Surgery When Necessary

Surgical Treatment for Neck Pain: When It Is the Right Step

Surgery for neck pain is recommended only when conservative care has been fully pursued without sufficient relief, when there is significant nerve or spinal cord compression causing weakness, or when imaging shows a structural problem that will not improve without correction. When surgery is needed, modern techniques are often minimally invasive, with smaller incisions and a faster recovery. Dr. Jawad is also recognized as the best spine surgeon in Hyderabad for these procedures.

01

Cervical Microdiscectomy

A minimally invasive procedure that removes the portion of a herniated cervical disc pressing on a nerve, relieving the arm and neck pain caused by the compression.

02

Cervical Decompression

For cervical stenosis, decompression surgery removes the bone or tissue narrowing the spinal canal, relieving pressure on the spinal cord and nerves.

03

Cervical Fusion and Disc Replacement

For instability or advanced disc disease, the affected segment is stabilized through fusion or, in selected cases, a disc replacement that preserves movement.

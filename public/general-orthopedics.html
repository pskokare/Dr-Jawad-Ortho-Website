What Conditions Does an Orthopedic Doctor in Hyderabad Treat?

Orthopedics covers every condition that affects the musculoskeletal system: bones, joints, cartilage, ligaments, tendons, muscles, and nerves. The following conditions are among the most commonly treated at this orthopaedic specialist hospital in Hyderabad.

Knee Conditions: Osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, cartilage damage, ligament injuries (ACL and PCL tears), meniscus tears, and knee instability. Patients requiring knee replacement can read about our joint replacement surgery and partial knee replacement options in detail.

Hip Conditions: Hip osteoarthritis, avascular necrosis (AVN) of the femoral head, hip dysplasia, and hip fractures requiring surgical management. Our hip replacement surgery page covers all hip surgical options.

Shoulder Conditions: Rotator cuff tears, shoulder instability, shoulder arthritis, frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), and shoulder fractures. Detailed information is available on our shoulder replacement surgery page.

Spine Conditions: Slip disc, sciatica, spinal stenosis, cervical and lumbar spondylosis, scoliosis, vertebral fractures, and degenerative disc disease. Dr. Jawad is also recognised as the best spine surgeon in Hyderabad for complex spinal procedures.

Sports Injuries: ACL tears, PCL tears, meniscal injuries, shoulder dislocations, cartilage lesions, and ligament injuries across all joints. Our arthroscopy and sports injuries service manages all sports-related musculoskeletal conditions.

Trauma and Fractures: Complex fractures of the long bones, pelvis, and periarticular regions, requiring surgical fixation and post-injury rehabilitation. Visit our trauma and fractures page for details.

Rheumatoid Arthritis: Inflammatory joint disease affecting multiple joints simultaneously. Medical and surgical management of rheumatoid arthritis is available through our rheumatoid arthritis service.

Foot and Ankle Conditions: Flatfoot deformity, ankle arthritis, Achilles tendon problems, bunions, and sports-related foot injuries. See our foot and ankle surgery page.

Hand and Wrist Conditions: Carpal tunnel syndrome, trigger finger, De Quervain's tenosynovitis, and hand fractures managed through our hand and microvascular surgery team.

Cartilage Injuries: Localised cartilage defects in the knee and other joints treated with OATS (Osteochondral Autograft Transfer System) or cartilage transplantation. Detailed information is on our cartilage transplantation and OATS procedure pages.

Surgical Procedures Performed by Top Orthopedic Surgeons in Hyderabad

The following surgical procedures are routinely performed at this facility. They represent the full range of what patients expect from the best orthopedic hospital in Hyderabad and the top orthopedic hospital in Hyderabad.

Total Knee Replacement: Replacement of a severely arthritic or damaged knee joint with a metal and polyethylene prosthesis. Robotic-assisted and computer-navigated techniques are available for greater precision in implant alignment.

Partial Knee Replacement: When only one compartment of the knee is damaged, partial knee replacement preserves healthy bone and tissue while replacing only the affected compartment, resulting in a more natural-feeling knee and faster recovery.

Total Hip Replacement: Replacement of a damaged hip joint with a prosthetic head and cup. Minimally invasive approaches are used wherever anatomy permits to reduce muscle trauma and recovery time.

Shoulder Replacement: Total and reverse shoulder replacement for severe shoulder arthritis, rotator cuff arthropathy, and complex proximal humerus fractures.

ACL and Ligament Reconstruction: Arthroscopic reconstruction of the ACL and other ligaments using autograft tissue, returning patients to full sporting activity with a structured rehabilitation programme.

Spine Surgery: Microdiscectomy, laminectomy, spinal fusion (TLIF, PLIF, ACDF), scoliosis correction, and vertebroplasty. Details of all spine surgical procedures are on our spine surgical treatment page.

Fracture Fixation: Open reduction and internal fixation (ORIF) of complex fractures using plates, screws, nails, and external fixators.

OATS and Cartilage Transplantation: Biological cartilage restoration procedures for localised joint cartilage defects in younger and active patients who wish to avoid or delay joint replacement.

Non-Surgical Treatments Available

Choosing the best orthopedic specialist in Hyderabad means choosing one whose first instinct is not always surgery. A meaningful proportion of orthopaedic conditions can be effectively managed without an operation. The following non-surgical options are offered at this orthopaedic specialist hospital in Hyderabad.

Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation: Customised programmes for every orthopaedic condition from post-fracture rehabilitation to joint preservation exercises for mild to moderate arthritis.

Image-Guided Injections: Corticosteroid injections, hyaluronic acid viscosupplementation, and PRP (platelet-rich plasma) injections for joint pain relief, all administered under ultrasound or fluoroscopic guidance.

ESWT (Extracorporeal Shock Wave Therapy): For chronic tendon pain, plantar fasciitis, lateral epicondylitis, rotator cuff tendinopathy, and back pain. Full details are on our ESWT page.

Stem Cell and PRP Injections: Biological regenerative therapies for cartilage degeneration and tendon pathology in suitable candidates.

RF Denervation: Radiofrequency ablation for chronic joint pain from facet joint and knee joint sources, providing long-lasting pain relief without surgery.

Alternative Treatments: For patients seeking non-conventional approaches to manage knee arthritis and back pain, our alternative treatments for knee arthritis and back problems page outlines additional options.

Pain Management: Our dedicated pain management team provides specialist interventional pain care for patients with chronic orthopaedic pain.
